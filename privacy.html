Your files are handled locally unless you intentionally use a web-based tool.

Overview

Morphit is designed to process images, documents, and PDF files on your device. Morphit does not sell your personal information and does not use third-party advertising trackers.

Photos, documents, and camera

Morphit accesses photos, documents, or the camera only when you choose to import, scan, edit, or export content. Processing is performed on your device. Exported files remain under your control.

Recent work

Morphit may save recent-work previews and reusable copies inside the app's local storage. You can remove this content from the app's settings. Deleting the app removes its local storage but does not remove files previously exported to Photos or Files.

Web tools

When you use Web to Image or Web to PDF, Morphit connects to the website address you provide. That website may receive standard network information, including your IP address, and is governed by its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Morphit uses StoreKit entitlement information to determine whether premium features are active.

Optional Sign in with Apple

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Morphit stores the identifier and any name or relay email you provide securely on your device. Morphit does not operate a separate account server. You can delete this local profile from Settings.

Data collection

Morphit does not currently collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or user-created files on developer-operated servers.

Children

Morphit is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Morphit's functionality or privacy practices change. The latest version will remain available on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email morphit.support@gmail.com.
