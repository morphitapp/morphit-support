Subscriptions

Purchases are managed by Apple. Open Morphit Settings to restore purchases or manage your subscription.

Account deletion

If you created an optional local profile, open Morphit → Settings → Account → Delete Account.

Removing recent work

Open Morphit → Settings → Storage → Clear Recent Work. Files already exported to Photos or Files must be deleted separately.
