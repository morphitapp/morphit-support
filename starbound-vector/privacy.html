STARBOUND: VECTOR · LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

Starbound: Vector is an independently developed game published under the Morphit name. It is designed to be enjoyed without creating an account with us. We do not sell personal information, serve advertising, track you across apps or websites, or use third-party analytics SDKs.

Information stored on your device

The game stores settings and gameplay progress locally on your device. This may include your high score, unlocked levels and ships, selected ship, collected ship parts, trail selection, difficulty, control preferences, tutorial status, and audio or accessibility settings.

This information is used only to operate the game and preserve your preferences. It is not transmitted to the developer. You can remove locally stored information by deleting the app, subject to standard iOS backup and restoration behaviour.

Game Center

If you choose to use Apple Game Center, the game may display your Game Center alias and submit eligible high scores to the global leaderboard. Game Center authentication, player identity and leaderboard services are provided by Apple and are governed by Apple’s privacy policy and your Game Center settings.

Starbound: Vector does not maintain its own account database and does not store your Game Center identity on a developer-operated server.

Motion controls

If you enable tilt controls, motion information from your device is processed temporarily on the device to control your spacecraft. The developer does not store or transmit this motion information.

Data collection and tracking

The current version of Starbound: Vector does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, cross-app tracking, or developer-operated cloud accounts. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players.

External services

Apple may process diagnostic, purchase, App Store, Game Center or device information independently under Apple’s terms and privacy practices. Those services are controlled by Apple rather than the developer of Starbound: Vector.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the game’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a material update is published.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to morphit.support@gmail.com.
