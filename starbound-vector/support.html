How can we help?

Send the details below and we’ll investigate.

Email support
01

Game Center

Confirm that you are signed into Game Center in iOS Settings, reopen the game, then use the trophy button. Scores waiting to sync will submit when authentication and connectivity are available.

02

Tilt controls

Open Settings inside the game, select Controls, choose Tilt, then hold your device in a comfortable neutral position. You can return to Drag controls at any time.

03

Audio

Check Music and Effects levels in the game’s Audio settings. Also confirm that your device volume is audible and that Silent Mode or a Focus mode is not affecting playback.

04

Performance

Install the newest game and iOS updates, close other demanding apps, and restart your device. When reporting a problem, include the device model, iOS version, game version and what happened immediately beforehand.
